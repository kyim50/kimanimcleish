## The idea

A portfolio is usually a scroll. Mine is a desktop: windows, a dock, a trash can and a wallpaper made from a 1745 map of Kingston. Each project is a **case file** you open like a document.

## What I would say here

- Why a desktop, and what it lets a visitor do that a scroll does not.
- The paste-up look: paper, torn edges and halftone prints.
- One thing that did not work, and what I changed.
