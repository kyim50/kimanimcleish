# Art credits

Pieces in this folder are cut, toned and torn from the sources below. Everything else is drawn from scratch for this site (ECG trace, watch, terminal, git graph, payment card, receipt, POS terminal, palette, canvas, hospital, document, checklist, browser).

## The owner's own work
- Cite app screens (public/work/cite/*): Cite, used by its owner.
- Tabi app screens (public/work/tabi/*): Tabi, used by its owner.
- Kingston plan and map prints (public/os/hay.webp, plan-1745.webp and the other public/os prints), the speakers and flyer art, the portrait (public/os/kimani.webp) and the resume page (public/os/resume-1.webp): the owner's own assets and public-domain prints.
